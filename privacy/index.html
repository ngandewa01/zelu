Zelu Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026. Applies to the Zelu expense tracker app for Android.

Your messages and payments stay on your phone. Zelu has no ads and no internet access, so it cannot send them anywhere.

On your phoneYour data stays here
No internetNo ads, no tracking
No loginNo account, no server

What Zelu reads

  • Zelu asks for permission to read and receive SMS. It uses this only to track your spending.
  • It looks only at messages from banks, cards, UPI apps and billers.
  • From those it picks out the amount, payee, account and due date.
  • It skips one-time passwords and personal chats.

Where your data is kept

  • Payments, categories, budgets and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • There is no account, no login and no server.
  • Your messages and payments are shared with no one, and are never sold or used for advertising.

No ads, no internet

  • Zelu shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.
  • Zelu does not ask for internet access, so it has no way to send anything off your phone by itself.

What leaves your phone, only when you choose

  • Backup: saved to Downloads/Zelu, locked with a password you set. Zelu cannot recover a forgotten password.
  • CSV export: a plain file in Downloads/Zelu. Delete it in your Files app when you are done.
  • Report a problem and crash reports: Zelu opens an email draft in your own mail app. You see everything in it and send it yourself. Long numbers and UPI names are hidden first, and crash details contain only technical error text.

Permissions

  • Read and receive SMS: to find payments in bank messages, as described above.
  • Notifications: payment alerts, daily and weekly summaries, and bill reminders, all made on your phone.
  • Run at startup: so reminders and the daily summary keep working after your phone restarts.

Deleting your data

  • In Zelu, open Menu, then Backup and security, then Erase everything. This clears all Zelu data.
  • Uninstalling the app does the same.
  • Your original text messages are never changed or deleted.
  • Backup and export files already in Downloads/Zelu stay until you delete them.

Children

  • Zelu is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

  • A new version will appear on this page with a new date, and in the app under Menu, then Help.
